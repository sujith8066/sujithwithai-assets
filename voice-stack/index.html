Build and test an AI voice agent almost for free

The stack I'd use, the startup credit programs that pay for it, who qualifies, and how to apply. Every amount is from the official program page, checked on 20 Oct 2026. Programs change, so check each page before you apply.

in credits for an early-stage startup that hasn't raised yet: LiveKit $23,000 + ElevenLabs ~$5,500 + Google $2,000 + Deepgram $1,000. That covers tens of thousands of test minutes.

The stack

LiveKit

Real-time audio and hosting for the agent
Up to $23,000
  • $6,000: the Scale plan free for 12 months, $10,000 extra cloud credits, $7,000 inference credits ($1,000 per partner model).
  • Who: voice, video or physical AI is core to the product; under 50 staff; under $5M raised and pre-Series A; incorporated; live website with named founders; in production or launching within 6 months.
  • Credits expire 12 months after they're issued. No pitch deck needed; the code arrives by email. A card on file is required.
livekit.com/startups

Deepgram

Hears the caller (speech-to-text) and detects when they've finished talking
Starts at $1,000, up to $100,000 as you grow
  • Their FAQ: the starter offer for direct applicants starts at $1,000; bigger tiers come with growth or through partner VCs and accelerators.
  • New accounts also get a $200 promo credit on sign-up.
  • Who: AI builders and early-stage startups in production or launching within 6 months; no agencies or consultants. Credits last 12 months.
deepgram.com/startup-program

Google for Startups (Gemini)

The brain: the language model that decides what to say
$2,000 before you raise; $200,000 to $350,000 with VC funding
  • Start tier ($2k): founded in the past 2 years, a working MVP, a clear business model, plans to raise venture funding soon.
  • Funded tiers need verifiable Pre-Seed to Series A funding from a known investor. AI startups: up to $250K in year 1 plus up to $100K in year 2.
  • Credits cover Google's own products, including Gemini. Answer usually in 5 to 7 days.
cloud.google.com/startup

ElevenLabs Grants

The voice (text-to-speech), plus their full agents platform
33,000,000 characters for 12 months (~680 hours of agent calls, "$5,500+ in value")
  • Who: startups with fewer than 25 employees, a business email and a business plan. No agencies or consulting firms. One application per company.
  • Decision within about a week.
elevenlabs.io/grants

The catch: the phone line

No program covers phone minutes (Twilio says it offers no startup credits). Test in the browser first: that's free. When you need a real phone number, budget for your carrier's per-minute rate.

Suggested order

  1. Build the first version in the browser on free tiers and sign-up credit.
  2. Register the company and put up a simple website with your names on it: most programs check.
  3. Apply to Deepgram, ElevenLabs, LiveKit and Google's Start tier in the same week.
  4. Only then buy a phone number and test with real callers.
