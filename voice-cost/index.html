What I'd take from this

  • Don't optimise the AI brain first: it's the smallest line. Hosting and the voice are where the money goes.
  • Platforms are worth it while you test an idea. Build your own stack once calls are steady.
  • Per-minute prices keep falling, so don't compete on minutes. Charge for the outcome: a booked appointment, a qualified lead.
  • Several prices are promotions: Gemini and Deepgram list rises or promo prices that end, so recheck before you set your price.
