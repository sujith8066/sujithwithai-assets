Data Deletion Instructions

Sujith With AI (Instagram: @sujithwithai) · Last updated 16 October 2026

The "Sujith With AI" app stores only the IDs of comments on @sujithwithai's own posts that it has already replied to. It does not store comment text, usernames, profile information or messages.

To delete any data related to you:

  1. Send a direct message to @sujithwithai on Instagram with the words "delete my data".
  2. We remove any stored comment IDs linked to your comments within 30 days.
  3. We confirm the deletion by replying to your message.

You can also delete your own comments on Instagram at any time. See our privacy policy.
