Privacy Policy

Sujith With AI (Instagram: @sujithwithai) · Last updated 16 October 2026

This policy explains what the "Sujith With AI" app does with Instagram data. The app is used only by the @sujithwithai Instagram account to publish its own reels and to reply to comments on its own posts.

What data we access

  • Comments posted on @sujithwithai's own posts: the comment text, the commenter's username and the comment ID.
  • Basic information about the @sujithwithai account and its own posts (for example view and like counts).

How we use it

  • To post a short public reply to a comment on our own post.
  • When someone comments a keyword we asked for in a reel (for example "PROMPT"), to send that person one private message with the link we promised in the reel.
  • The text of a comment may be sent to an AI service (Google Gemini) only to draft the short reply. It is not used for any other purpose.

What we store

We store only the IDs of comments we have already replied to, so we never reply twice. We do not store comment text, usernames, profile information or messages. We do not sell, rent or share Instagram data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Your choices and data deletion

You can delete your comment on Instagram at any time. To ask us to delete any data related to you, send a direct message to @sujithwithai on Instagram with the words "delete my data". We will remove any stored comment IDs linked to you within 30 days and confirm by message. See also our data deletion instructions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: send a direct message to @sujithwithai on Instagram.
