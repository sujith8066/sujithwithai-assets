How I'd position a new one

  • Pick a niche where a missed call has a clear dollar value and the agent must write into that niche's system of record (PMS, EHR, DMS, FSM, CRM).
  • Name the job, not the technology: e.g. "AI leasing agent for self-storage", not "voice AI platform".
  • Price per outcome or per unit, such as per booked showing, per resolved maintenance call or per door, with a floor. Avoid pure per-minute pricing.
  • Lead with an ROI number from a named customer, and show the escalation path to a human.
  • Make integrations and compliance the moat: certified integrations, TCPA-safe consent flows for outbound, HIPAA or fair-housing guardrails where relevant.
  • Expand from voice into the workflow: texts, email, scheduling, payments. a16z: "voice will become the wedge, not the product."
