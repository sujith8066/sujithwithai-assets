The catches

Vercel's free plan is personal use only. Its docs say "Hobby teams are restricted to non-commercial personal use only." Taking payments or advertising a product counts as commercial, so you'd need a paid plan. Source (updated 14 Sep 2026).
Supabase pauses idle free projects. "Free projects are paused after 1 week of inactivity." Fine while you're building; plan for it if your app can go quiet for a week. Source
Free AI coding runs on monthly caps. Copilot Free has 2,000 completions and limited chat a month. Google replaced its free Gemini CLI with Antigravity CLI on 18 June 2026 and doesn't publish the free limits as a number.
Payment fees aren't zero. Stripe has no monthly fee, but it takes a cut of each sale. That's the good kind of cost: you only pay it when someone pays you.

The real cost

Money won't stop your launch. Your time is the real cost. Spend it finding your first 10 customers.
