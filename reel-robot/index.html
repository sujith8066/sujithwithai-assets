How my reel robot works

Every morning an AI pipeline turns fresh AI news into a finished Instagram reel. I review it and say "go". Nothing is posted without that. Here is the whole setup, with real numbers from my reel of 17 Oct 2026.

One real run (17 Oct 2026): news scan 7:43, topic picked 7:48, voice 7:50, final visuals 8:12, ready to post 8:17. 34 minutes, and 9 HeyGen credits (with the avatar; reels without it use about 2).

The 6 steps

  1. Read the news. A small Python script reads 98 sources (company blogs like OpenAI, Anthropic and Google, Hacker News, GitHub Trending, Techmeme, Indian tech news) and lists everything from the last 48 hours. A simple scoring rule ranks them: big name, usable today, free, money or jobs, India angle.
  2. Check the facts. The AI opens the original page (not a news article about it), saves the text and screenshots the exact sentence. If a number can't be found at the source, it doesn't go in the reel.
  3. Write and voice it. A 25 to 40 second script, with the big number in the first line. HeyGen speaks it in my cloned voice and returns the timing of every word.
  4. Animate in code. Each scene is an HTML page. A headless browser (Playwright) renders it frame by frame, timed to the words, and ffmpeg adds music (ElevenLabs), sound effects and captions.
  5. A second AI reviews it. Gemini watches the finished video with sound and scores 14 things (hook, captions, readability, audio). Fixes get made before I see it.
  6. Reply to comments. A GitHub Actions job runs every hour. Keyword comments get a DM with the link; other comments get a short friendly reply written by Gemini. Spam gets skipped.

What I learned so far

  • The first second decides everything: Instagram shows each reel to about 100 strangers first. In my numbers, when more than half of them swiped away, the reel stopped there.
  • Use 5 hashtags at most. With more, Instagram's API published my reels with no caption at all.
  • Real proof beats nice graphics: screenshots of the original source, real numbers, real output.
  • Keep a human in the loop. I still approve every post.
