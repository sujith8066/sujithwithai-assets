Why this matters

Anthropic's IPO filing (Form S-1, filed with the SEC on 26 Oct 2026) says it spent $944 million on computing and infrastructure in January to March 2025, against $376 million of revenue: about $2.51 for every $1. By April to June 2026 that was 48% of revenue, with a $1.0 billion operating profit. The bill still grew, to $5.6 billion a quarter, but revenue grew faster, to $11.6 billion. It buys computing 12 to 18 months ahead, so that cost is fixed in the near term. The filing (pages 159 and 160).
Most AI apps work the other way: you pay for every request, so every new user adds to the bill. That's why the number above matters from your first paying user.
