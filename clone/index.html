What it couldn't build

  • Trust. Reviews from real guests, ID checks, and a team to call when a stay goes wrong.
  • Supply. Airbnb says it has over 5.5 million hosts and 9 million+ active listings (news.airbnb.com, May 2026). This app has six made-up ones.
  • Money. Real payments, payouts to hosts in 220+ countries and regions, refunds, fraud checks.
  • The rules. Permits, taxes and short-stay limits that change city by city.
  • Everything behind the screen. Accounts, a database, messaging, search ranking, scale. This demo is front-end only.

The takeaway

AI made the app the easy part. Getting hosts, guests and trust is still the hard part, and that's the business. If you're a founder, spend your time there.
